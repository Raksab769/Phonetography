The Eye Behind the Lens

Baskar S

Phonetography is the practice of treating the smartphone camera not as a convenience, but as a serious instrument. Baskar S has spent years refining this discipline — studying light the way a cinematographer would, composing with the rigour of a print photographer, and post-processing with restraint.

Based in South India, the work spans street portraiture, architectural abstraction, landscape minimalism, and documentary series. Each project begins with a single question: what does this scene demand? The answer determines everything that follows — angle, moment, exposure, edit.

Approach

Light, geometry, and the decisive second.

Phonetography is not defined by the device — it is defined by the discipline behind it. The smartphone imposes creative constraints that sharpen compositional instinct: no interchangeable optics, no bulky presence, no intimidating apparatus between photographer and subject. What remains is pure observation.

I Observe before shooting
II Light is the subject
III Restraint over excess
IV Truth over spectacle
